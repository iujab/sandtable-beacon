Beacon SOS for iPhone

If you are trapped in a building on fire, this app tells the fire crew that you are there, how many people are with you, and whether you can move. The pucks they throw into the room find your phone.

What it does

  1. You open the app and press what is happening: trapped, injured, sheltering, or “I am out”.
  2. Your phone sends an SOS beacon over Bluetooth with that status, the number of people with you, your battery and your floor.
  3. On an iPhone 11 or later, the rescue pucks also measure their distance to your phone over UWB. Several pucks, or your phone held up and pointed at the door, turn that into a position in the room.
  4. The app shows you what the rescuers know: how many beacons hear you, and how well they can place you.

The privacy promise

  • Nothing is sent until you open the app and choose to share.
  • Your phone is known by a short code that changes, never by your name, number or a hardware address.
  • Phones without the app are only counted as nearby devices. They are never identified or stored.
