# Beacon on the command board — contract addendum (lead, 12:15 CST)

**The product is now "Sandtable Beacon".** Owner direction at 12:10: victims are located by their phones, and this project is the command and reporting centre that shows them in the room, on a map made from the CoStar, Matterport and Vexcel layers. Three sensing modes share one board:

| Mode | What it finds | What it needs | Status this weekend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beacon** (headline) | occupants, by their phones | pucks with Bluetooth and UWB (Qorvo DWM3001CDK) thrown into the room; an opt-in app on the phone for a ring or a fix; without the app only a device count | built by sibling lane L16 in `../sandtable-beacon-victim-phones/` (port 1885); real hardware targeted |
| Scout | an untagged, breathing person; a coarse echo outline | several pucks reading channel impulse responses | **simulation only**: the echo-reading firmware reused from lane L08 is for ESP32 boards and the owner's boards are nRF52833 + DW3110 |
| Crews | firefighters carrying tags | anchors and tags | **simulation only** for the same reason; the mayday exercise keeps working as it is |

The Beacon module's feed is frozen in `../sandtable-beacon-victim-phones/docs/BEACON_FEED.md` (**read it fully before writing code against it**; its section 0 is the physics that must not be over-claimed: a ring is a ring, two candidates are two candidates, no `x`/`y` unless `geometry` is `"fix"`, devices are not people). Its sample is `../sandtable-beacon-victim-phones/fixtures/beacon/sample_beacons.json`. That directory is another team's: never edit it. `docs/SCOUT_CONTRACT.md` section 3 (rules, ports, reports) still applies to everyone.

## 1. Server side (builder R3: `sandtable/beacon_link.py` plus `server.py`, `incident.py`, `director.py`)

- `BeaconLink`: reads the Beacon server at `BEACON_URL` (default `http://127.0.0.1:1885`): `GET /api/beacons` (poll at about 2 Hz, or its stream), `GET /api/brief`. It must tolerate the server being absent, slow or restarted (state `connected: false`, no exceptions, retry). `SANDTABLE_BEACON` = `auto` (default: use the server when it answers), `off`, or `sample` (replay a copy of the sample object kept at `fixtures/beacon/sample_beacons.json` in this tree, everything marked `"sample": true`, for development and for running with no Beacon server).
- WebSocket `/ws`: `{"type": "beacons", ...the Beacons object unchanged..., "link": {"connected", "url", "age_s", "sample"}}` whenever it changes, and to new clients after `hello`. `state` gains `"beacon": {"connected", "source", "simulated", "phones", "fixes", "people_reported"}`.
- HTTP: `GET /api/beacons` (the latest relayed object or null), `POST /api/beacon/pucks` (forwards puck positions to the Beacon server's `POST /api/pucks`; the scout scan's `pucks[]` are forwarded automatically when a scan lands), `GET /api/accountability`, `GET /api/report`, `GET /api/report.md`.
- **Accountability** (`sandtable.accountability/1`): the Beacon object's own `accountability` (the building record's suites against phones) joined with what only this board knows: crews in each room, hazards, radar victims, and who was assigned to and reached whom. `{"type": "accountability", "floor", "rows": [{"suite", "room", "room_label", "tenant", "life_hazard", "expected": {...}, "phones_opt_in", "people_reported", "located", "cold_devices", "unaccounted": {...}, "state", "priority", "crews": ["RIT"], "assigned": "RIT" | null, "reached": bool, "out": bool}], "totals": {...}, "source": {...}, "limits": [...]}`. Emitted on `/ws` as `accountability` when it changes.
- **Incident**: phones are people-shaped targets like scout victims. Situation `phone` when an opt-in phone first becomes actionable (a fix, candidates, a ring or a zone, or a report of `trapped` or `injured`), and again when its geometry improves to a fix or its report changes; `data` is the discrete brief (from the Beacon server's `/api/brief`, trimmed), never raw floats or timestamps. `assign(unit, "rescue", "B1")`: a fix routes to the nearest graph node; candidates route to the most probable one and say so; a ring or zone routes to the door of the most probable room and says "search". `phone_reached` when the crew arrives (a fix: at the point; otherwise: in the room). The phone's people leave with the crew on `exit`; `all_out` ends the incident. `snapshot()` gains `"phones"` only when phones exist, so the recordings of the mayday exercise keep matching.
- **Feeds**: `/feed/scan.geojson` and `/feed/cot.xml` also carry the phones (a fix as a point with its uncertainty; a ring as a ring: a GeoJSON LineString or Polygon for the arcs and a CoT event at the puck with the radius in `ce` and the word "ring" in the remarks; never a made-up point).
- **Report** (`sandtable.report/1`), what a command post keeps: `{"type": "report", "generated_t", "simulated", "incident": {"address", "phase", "elapsed_s"}, "building": {...the record facts used and the missing ones...}, "provenance": {...per layer: real, fixture or simulated, and the Beacon source...}, "people": [{"id", "source": "phone" | "radar" | "crew", "status", "people", "where", "geometry", "located_t_rel", "assigned", "reached_t_rel", "out_t_rel", "state"}], "accountability": {...}, "crews": [...], "hazards": [...], "timeline": [...], "agent": {"preplan_summary", "aar"}, "limits": [...]}`; `/api/report.md` is the same as Markdown.
- **Exercise script** `beacon` (the new default; `scout` and `mayday` stay): reset here and on the Beacon server, alarm, pre-plan, Engine 1 to the fire room's door, pucks go in (`POST /api/pucks` on the Beacon server with the scout simulator's puck positions when available, else `{"n": 3}`), the Beacon server's scripted phones answer (`POST /api/sim/story` or `POST /api/sim/phone`; read its contract), situation `phone`, the agent routes the rescue team, `phone_reached`, the way out, all out, the report. Condition-based with timeouts, as the other scripts. With no Beacon server, the script runs on the `sample` source and says so on screen.

## 2. Agent (builder R4)

Tools `get_phones()` (the discrete brief: each phone's id, geometry, where in words, report, confidence, and the limits) and `get_accountability()`; `route`/`assign` accept `B1`; situations `phone` and `phone_reached` as goals with a reply budget; the after-action task for Beacon incidents includes the accountability table and asks for a hand-off report a command post would keep (who was reported, who was found, who is still unaccounted for and why that number is a range). The agent must respect the geometry: it may not send a crew to "the phone's position" when there is only a ring; it searches the arc or the room. Existing prompts stay byte-identical.

## 4. Accountability against the record (lead, 13:40 CST)

What a run on a real record showed: a scan is often of space that is for lease, so the record may say the scanned suite has no tenant while phones report people in it. That is a finding, not missing data, and the panel, the agent and the report must say it on purpose: "the record says this suite is empty; the phones say three people are in it". The storey's other tenancies are the neighbours still to account for.

The rule: **the building record is this board's to read; the phones are the Beacon module's.** When the record layer is live (`labels.costar.mode == "live"`), `beacon_link.accountability()` takes the phone columns from the Beacon object and every record column from the provider. In fixture mode nothing changes: no new keys, same values, so the recorded agent turns keep matching (the replay tests are the check).

Additions in live mode (`sandtable.accountability/1`, additive):

- One row per suite: the rooms of one suite are merged (`rooms` lists them all, `room` is the first, `room_label` is "Suite N (k rooms)"); phone and crew columns are summed over the rooms. Rooms without a suite stay one row each.
- `scope`: `"scan"` (rooms in the twin) or `"storey"` (a tenancy of the same storey in the record whose rooms are not in the twin; `room` is null).
- `record`: `{"status": "tenant" | "available" | "vacant", "tenants": n, "employees": int | null, "area_sf": int | null, "text": "..."}`. `available`: no current tenant and the suite is among the record's available spaces; `vacant`: no current tenant and not listed. Added 13:55: `"unnamed"`: one or more occupancy entries on record, none with a tenant name (expected people null, basis "an occupancy entry on record with no tenant named and no head count"; the row's state is what the phones make it, never `against-record`, which is only for `available` or `vacant`; `record_vs_phones` is emitted for it too).
- `covered`: true when a puck stands in one of the row's rooms; always false for `scope: "storey"`.
- `expected`: employees in the record when present (the existing rule: low half, high 1.2 times), basis "employees in the building record"; a tenant without a head count: `people` null, basis "tenant on record, head count not in the record"; no tenant: 0, basis "no current tenant on the record".
- `state` gains `"against-record"` (people reported, or devices heard, in a suite the record has no tenant for) and `"not-covered"` (a tenancy on record with no puck in it: nobody there is accounted for, `unaccounted` is the expected range).
- `why`: one sentence from discrete facts, for example "the record lists this suite as available space with no tenant; 2 phones report 3 people in it".
- Top level: `headline` (one or two sentences a commander reads first), `storey` `{"label", "tenancies", "in_scan", "not_covered", "expected_low", "expected_high"}`; `totals` gain `against_record_people`, `not_covered_suites`; `source.real_sponsor_data` and `source.text` come from the provider's labels, not from the Beacon module's.
- `priorities`: an `against-record` row with people in it comes first.
- The report's `differences` gains `{"kind": "record_vs_phones", "source": ["costar", "beacon"], "text": headline}` when there is such a row.
- Agent: `get_accountability()` in live mode returns the headline, the storey summary and the compact rows; the after-action facts include the headline. Counts only in prompts: no tenant names beyond what the tool already returns.
- Board: the panel opens with the headline; the scanned suite's row shows the record's status next to what the phones say; the storey's other tenancies are one collapsible group "Same storey, no pucks there: N tenancies, about L to H people on record".
